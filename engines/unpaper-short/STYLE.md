# Unpaper: visual style & context (user's brief, 2026-11-22)

## Positioning
"Jo document sab share karte hain, par koi padhta nahi, hum 60 sec mein padh ke batate hain."
Budget, RBI policy, SEBI circulars, court rulings, new rules, company results, research papers. Showing the real source is the USP and builds trust.

## Visual (3D Unpaper world)
- Paper Lab: a grey studio with floating 3D paper sheets, soft shadows and an orange key light.
- Hook (0-2s): a document spins in in 3D, its orange corner peels, and a big number pops out from inside.
- Decode shot: the camera dives into the page, an orange highlighter sweeps a line, and that line lifts up as 3D text.
- Proof chip: a dark pill on every claim with source · page · date.
- 3D props in a matte clay style: rupee coin stack, gavel, house, chart bars. Only 2-3 per video.
- Outro: the paper folds into the logo, with "SUBSCRIBE · Unpaper" and a next-video teaser.
- Mascot option: an origami paper character as the series identity.

## Content mix (weekly)
- 5-7 Shorts/Reels (30-60 sec): "Is rule se aapki jeb par kya asar?"
- 1-2 long-form (6-12 min): one big document fully decoded.
- Series: "Fine Print", "Budget Decoded", "Rule Badla".
- One question, one answer per video. A different story each time on the same template.

## Script formula
1. Hook: a shocking number, or "aapko yeh pata hona chahiye".
2. Show the document: what it is.
3. Decode 3 points.
4. "Aap par asar": the personal angle.
5. CTA: a question for the comments + subscribe + next part.

## CTA & engagement
- "Kaunsa document decode karein? Comment karo." Make the next video on that, and show the commenter's name on screen.
- Pinned comment: the source link and the Instagram link.
- A poll or Community post every 3 days.

## 3-platform connection
- YouTube: long-form + Shorts. This is the main earning engine.
- Instagram: the same Shorts as Reels, carousels ("5 points in 5 slides") and Stories polls. YouTube link in bio.
- Third platform (Facebook Page "Unpaper"; X or Telegram optional): a 1-line summary + link for each document.
- Every video ends with: "Poora breakdown YouTube par / slides Instagram par".

## Money (realistic order)
1. Views and subs come from 30-60 days of consistency.
2. Brand deals and affiliate (finance apps, books) are possible even before YPP.
3. YPP brings ads, then memberships.
4. Later: a paid newsletter or PDF summaries.

## Don't
- Bought subs, sub4sub or bots.
- Clickbait the video doesn't deliver.
- Reposting the same video with light changes.
