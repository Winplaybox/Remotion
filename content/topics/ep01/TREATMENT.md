# TREATMENT.md — Ep01 (v2 — engagement rewrite)

**Style:** Exploded Teardown (3D Layer Stack)
**Target:** 120s · 30fps = 3600 frames (duration driven by actual narration audio)
**Structure:** Hook → Curiosity Gap → Escalation → Payoff → CTA

## Why v1 failed
- No hook — opened with a flat statement, no tension
- No "what if" / stakes — viewer had no reason to keep watching
- Visuals were just fade-ins, no real motion design
- No audio — text-only scroll kills retention
- No CTA — video just ended

## v2 Script Beats (narration-timed, Hinglish)

| Beat | Time | VO Line (Hinglish) | Visual |
|------|------|---------------------|--------|
| Hook | 0–8s | "Agar main tumse kahu ki ek machine ne, sirf 5 saal mein, wo kaam seekh liya jisme insaan ko sadiyo lagi — tum believe karoge?" | Black screen, single glowing stat counter spins up |
| Curiosity gap | 8–18s | "Aur sabse darawani baat? Ye sirf shuruat hai. Jo tum abhi dekhoge, usko Stanford ki official 2026 report ne record kiya hai." | Title slam, source badge stamps in |
| Escalation 1 | 18–40s | "2019 mein AI ek photo tak sahi se pehchaan nahi sakti thi. 2024 mein, usne MMLU test mein 90% score kiya — matlab law, medicine, science ke sawalo mein almost insaan jaisa." | Benchmark plates rise, counter animates 0→90% |
| Escalation 2 | 40–60s | "Lekin yahi nahi — 2023 mein akele 149 naye AI models launch hue. Matlab har 2-3 din mein ek naya AI duniya mein aaya." | Model-count bar explodes upward, frame-synced ticks |
| Escalation 3 | 60–80s | "Companies ne is race mein $67 billion dollar laga diye — sirf ek saal mein. Ye paisa kisi normal business mein nahi, balki ek hathiyaro ki race jaisa lag raha hai." | Investment dial sweeps, particle burst on peak |
| What-if turn | 80–95s | "Toh sawaal ye nahi ki AI kitni smart hai. Sawaal ye hai — agar yahi speed rahi, to 2030 tak tumhari job, tumhari padhai, sab kuch kaisa dikhega?" | Screen splits: today vs 2030 silhouette |
| Payoff | 95–108s | "Benchmarks khatam ho rahe hain — AI insaan se aage nikal chuki hai kai jagah. Ab asli sawaal competition nahi, control ka hai." | Insight card, slow zoom, dim lighting |
| CTA | 108–120s | "Agar tumhe lagta hai ye sirf shuruat hai, to COMMENT karo 'AI' — aur FOLLOW karo, kyuki part 2 mein hum dikhayenge AI ki asli chhupi hui cost." | Animated subscribe button, comment prompt, channel logo |

## Visual upgrade notes
- Replace flat fade-ins with: spring-driven scale + 3D perspective tilt on plates
- Add kinetic captions (word-by-word reveal synced to audio) — required for Shorts retention
- Add particle/glow accents on big numbers for "wow" moment
- Split-screen comparison for the "what-if" beat (today vs 2030)
- Hard cut every 3-5s minimum — no scene longer than 20s without a visual change

## CTA requirements
- Explicit ask: comment a specific word (boosts algorithm engagement signal)
- Explicit ask: follow for named next-episode hook (creates series stickiness)
- On-screen animated subscribe icon + channel handle
