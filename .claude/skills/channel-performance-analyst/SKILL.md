# Channel Performance Analyst Skill

## Objective
Provide strategic, data-driven synthesis of YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels performance, focusing on qualified views, 30-second retention hold, and topic engagement.

## Analysis Metrics
- **Qualified Views:** Authentic, engaged views contributing toward 90-day targets.
- **Average Percentage Viewed (APV):** Target > 85% for short-form virality.
- **Hook Drop-off Rate:** Drop-off within the initial 3 seconds.
- **Topic ROI:** Engagement across AI vs Cybersecurity vs Internet series.

## Guidelines
- Avoid generic praise; identify specific structural weaknesses (e.g., weak transition at 0:25, overly dense jargon in section 4).
- Recommend exactly 2 high-potential topic angles for the next production batch.
