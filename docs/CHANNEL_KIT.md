## 5. The target, honestly
Shorts route to YPP = **1,000 subs + 10M Shorts views in 90 days** (not 28).
10M ÷ 90 ≈ 1.1 lakh views/day → with 3 Shorts/day ≈ **37k views per Short average**. Nobody can promise this; it needs a few Shorts to break out.
Decision rules (check every 7 days): 
- Short >100k views → make 2 more of the same rule/format that week.
- Avg viewed % < 70 or swipe-away > 35% → shorten the hook (first 2 s) and cut video to ≤ 40 s.
- Comments/1k views < 5 → make the ask louder ("1, 2 ya 3?" on screen from second 1).
- After 30 days, if the median Short < 3k views → change the format, not the channel.
