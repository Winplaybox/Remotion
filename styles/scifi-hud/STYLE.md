# Sci-Fi Hologram HUD (`scifi-hud`)
*Inspired by high-tech JARVIS military telemetry, cybernetic terminals, and anime HUD interfaces.*

## 1. Aesthetic Atmosphere
- **Mood:** Highly technical, fast-paced, precision-engineered, classified military AI.
- **Background:** Deep cyber void (`#020B0B`) with emerald/cyan gradient core (`#062226`).
- **Cards & Surfaces:** Hexagonal HUD panels (`rgba(6, 34, 38, 0.85)`) with electric cyan borders (`#00FFCC`), crosshairs, and corner telemetry ticks.
- **Visual Motifs:** Live floating coordinate tickers, rotating target reticles, digital scanlines (`repeating-linear-gradient`).

## 2. Typography
- **Primary Font:** Monospace Engineering (`Consolas`, `Courier New`, `monospace`).
- **Headlines:** Weight 800, uppercase, letter spacing `+1.5px`.
- **Telemetry Readouts:** Bracketed counters `[TENSOR_DIM: 12288 // STATUS: OPTIMAL]`.

## 3. Camera Dynamics
- **Motion:** Parallax orbit with responsive mouse/spring pan (`zoom: 1.08`).
- **Shake / Hit:** Subtle 2px high-frequency jitter on technical keyword hits.

## 4. Lemo-Wake Layering
- **Diagrams:** Wireframe holographic projection with glowing emerald data buses, animated vector arrows, and laser sweeping scanner.
- **Presenter Avatar:** Holographic cybernetic AI operator with scanline glitch overlay, audio-reactive energy aura, and natural eye blinks.
