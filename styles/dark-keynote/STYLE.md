# Dark Tech Keynote (`dark-keynote`)
*Inspired by high-production Apple Keynotes and Vercel Ship events.*

## 1. Aesthetic Atmosphere
- **Mood:** Premium, architectural, authoritative, clean, expensive.
- **Background:** Deep obsidian (`#030712`) with radial spotlight (`#0B132B`) centered at 50% X, 40% Y.
- **Cards & Surfaces:** Frosted glassmorphic panels (`rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.75)`) with 1.5px cyber cyan glowing border (`rgba(0, 245, 255, 0.35)`) and 24px rounded corners.
- **Lighting:** Subdued cyan (`#00F5FF`) and ultraviolet (`#7928CA`) ambient volumetric drop shadows.

## 2. Typography
- **Primary Font:** Modern Sans-Serif (`Segoe UI`, `Inter`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`).
- **Headlines:** Ultra-bold (900 weight), uppercase, letter spacing `-1.5px`.
- **Accents:** Dual-tone gradient text (`linear-gradient(135deg, #00F5FF 0%, #7928CA 100%)`).

## 3. Camera Dynamics
- **Motion:** Slow, cinematic push-in (`scale: 1.0 -> 1.05`) over the composition duration.
- **Tilt:** Damped 3D perspective (`perspective: 1200px`) with subtle oscillation.

## 4. Lemo-Wake Layering
- **Diagrams:** High-contrast dark cards with cyan vector streams and golden mathematical annotations.
- **Presenter Avatar:** Sophisticated tech architect portrait with ambient cyan rim glow, natural breathing, and speech-synced lighting.
