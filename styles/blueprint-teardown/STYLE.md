# Exploded Teardown & Blueprint (`blueprint-teardown`)
*Inspired by patent schematics, industrial CAD blueprints, and hardware component teardowns.*

## 1. Aesthetic Atmosphere
- **Mood:** Analytical, industrial, structural, rigorous engineering.
- **Background:** Technical cobalt blueprint blue (`#0B1D3A`) with sub-grid (`#122A54`) and fine grid lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Cards & Surfaces:** Patent callout boxes (`rgba(15, 37, 72, 0.9)`) with white architectural borders and technical dimension calipers.
- **Visual Motifs:** Exploded axis lines, assembly part numbers (FIG. 2A, PART #884), cross-section indicators.

## 2. Typography
- **Primary Font:** Industrial Geometric (`Franklin Gothic Medium`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`).
- **Headlines:** Weight 700, uppercase, letter spacing `0.5px`.
- **Dimensions:** Subscript annotations (`±0.002mm`, `Δt < 1.2ns`).

## 3. Camera Dynamics
- **Motion:** Lateral pan across schematic axes (`pan_left`, `zoom: 1.04`).

## 4. Lemo-Wake Layering
- **Diagrams:** Animated 3D blueprint with mechanical exploded layers floating outwards along the Z-axis.
- **Presenter Avatar:** Blueprint draftsman / systems architect silhouette with architectural measurement calipers.
